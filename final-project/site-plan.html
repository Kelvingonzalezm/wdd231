Site Plan

Site Name

Guayaquil Food Guide

This name was chosen because it clearly represents a website focused on the food culture, traditional dishes, and restaurants of Guayaquil, Ecuador.

Site Purpose

This website will provide information about traditional food, popular restaurants, and local dining experiences in Guayaquil. Visitors will be able to explore different dishes, discover recommended restaurants, and learn more about the city's culinary culture through interactive features and dynamic content.

Scenarios

  • What are the most popular traditional dishes in Guayaquil?
  • Which restaurants are recommended for visitors who want to try local food?

Color Scheme

  • Dark Blue (#1F3A5F): Used for headings, navigation, and important elements.
  • Golden Yellow (#F48400): Used for buttons, highlights, and accents.
  • White (#FFFFFF): Used as the main background color.

Typography

Roboto: This font will be used for all headings, body text, navigation, and other content because it is clean, modern, and easy to read.
